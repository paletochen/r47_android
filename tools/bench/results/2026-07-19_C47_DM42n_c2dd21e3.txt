Observations from this run:
- The hardware-tax factor (hardware s / host s) spans 81x (BMTRIG, where
  decNumber compute dominates) to 369x (BMREG, nearly all per-step
  interpreter dispatch) on USB power. For ordinary program loops the cost
  a hardware user feels is fixed per-step overhead, not arithmetic.
- Battery is not a uniform 2x of USB despite the halved clock: BMREG and
  BMDISP scale at 1.9x, BMVAR at 2.6x, BMTRIG at 3.2x. Compute-bound
  paths lose more than the clock ratio on battery.
- The lookup diagnostic (BMVAR - BMREG) / (BMRSV - BMREG) is 4.7x on USB,
  4.2x on battery, 4.9x on the host: the linear user-variable scan costs
  about five times the hashed reserved-variable path per access, in every
  environment (upstream issue #555).
