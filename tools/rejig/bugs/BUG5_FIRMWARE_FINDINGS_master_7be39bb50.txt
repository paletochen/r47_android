THE SHAPE THAT IS WRONG
-----------------------

_executeOp case PARAM_LABEL (programming/lblGtoXeq.c) passes a local label 00 to 99 and A to l as the raw
operand byte, 0 to 123. fnGoto, fnExecute and fnCheckLabel all test label <= LAST_LOCAL_LABEL first and
search the current program. Six functions do not:

    solve.c        fnPgmSlv            PGMSLV, and PGMPLT through fnPgmPlt
    integrate.c    fnPgmInt            PGMINT
    differentiate.c derivativeCommon   f'(x), f"(x), and PGMDRV on the derivative branch
    sumprod.c      _checkArgument      SUMn, PRODn
    isumprod.c     _checkiArgument     iSUMn, iPRODn
    input.c        fnVarMnu            VARMNU, and fn42VarMnu

The first five test FIRST_LABEL to LAST_LABEL (2200 to 6999) and then REGISTER_X to REGISTER_T (100 to 103).
A numeric label falls past both and reaches the out of range branch. A lettered label A to D IS 100 to 103,
so it is read as a stack register and the letter is looked up as a GLOBAL label name: with no such label the
run stops, with one it runs the wrong program and says nothing. E to L land out of range.

fnVarMnu has no range test at all: _isVarMenu indexes labelList[label - FIRST_LABEL], and for label 1 that
subtraction wraps, so the read is far outside the array.


THE STEP DECODER DISAGREES WITH THE ENCODER
-------------------------------------------

Keying SOLVE and then the A key in PEM stores operand 100 and the calculator lists the step as "SOLVE X".
TAM writes 100 for the local label A (ui/tam.c, the LOCAL_LABEL arm of registerLookup), while SOLVE carries
PTP_REGISTER so the decoder renders 100 as the register X. Measured base behaviour: that step selects the
global label 'X'. Under the candidate fix it selects the local label A, which is what was typed. The listing
still prints "SOLVE X"; that decoder is not touched by the patch.


THE CANDIDATE FIX
-----------------

One helper, findLocalLabel in programming/manage.c, returning the label list index of a numeric or lettered
local label of the current program and reporting ERROR_LABEL_NOT_FOUND itself. Five lines at each of the six
sites, plus a bounds test in _isVarMenu. In derivativeCommon the resolution goes ahead of setSystemFlag so
the early return cannot leak FLAG_SOLVING.

The corpus cases solve_cov.txt and deriv_cov.txt assert EC=6 for fnPgmSlv(REGISTER_T) and fn1stDeriv
(REGISTER_T). The helper reports ERROR_LABEL_NOT_FOUND, which is EC=6, so both keep their expected code.
The full test suite was not run: this is a candidate for discussion, not a settled package.

After the fix the REGISTER_X to REGISTER_T arm of the five label-only functions is unreachable.
